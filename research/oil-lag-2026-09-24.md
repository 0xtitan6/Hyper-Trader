## What that means for the lag question

You cannot measure a lag against the underlying at 37 trades/day. The prior
barrier lag test (`research/barrier-lag-2026-09-22`) used the HYPE $100 touch at
3,562 trades/24h and collected 50 minutes of tape (29,431 perp prints, 1,680
book snapshots) to get 8 tradeable stale-ask signals — of which 1/8 was a
winning round trip. Sample count matters.

Scaling that ratio down to xyz:CL: 37/3562 ≈ 1%. Extrapolated, a comparable
tape session would produce **≤1 tradeable signal, well below any statistical
threshold**. The lag might exist — but we cannot observe it, and even if we
could, the flow ceiling is a few dollars a day.

The one liquid book (oid=1230, `out` venue) is not a lag surface. It is a deep
out-of-the-money barrier (83.196 vs current 92.23) trading at ~92% probability
five days out. Prints happen when holders cash out, not because the book
mis-tracks the underlying. Selling YES here is short-vol with a 30-cent tail
against 8-cent premium — the same 33:1 shape MEASURED.md rejected under
"BTC 95k strike".

## What I assumed, and what it costs

- `candleSnapshot n` counts fills, not orders. Verified against
  `recentTrades` for oid=1230: 10 recent trades all within 24h, consistent with
  the 555 rate. `recentTrades` caps at 10 so it under-counts busy markets — the
  candle numbers are the reliable figure.
- Notional = `sum(v * (h+l)/2)` where v is base (shares) and (h+l)/2 approximates
  the traded price. On probability-priced markets this is dollars staked, which
  is what "notional" means here.
- The `perp:xyz:CL` descriptor covers only the 5 outcomes above. Did not survey
  `template:priceTouch` on xyz:CL — none appeared in `outcomeMeta`.

## Verdict

Structurally the same result as crypto barriers, one rung lower on the flow
ladder. HYPE/BTC barriers were closed because the incumbents requote in 3.8s at
$2 of size — we could not compete. xyz:CL binaries have the opposite problem:
nothing to compete for. 37 trades a day cannot support a maker book that pays
back the cost of running it.

**Do not build a repricer for `perp:xyz:CL`.** If the crypto barrier build ever
proves out at the fast tick, revisit only if perp:xyz:CL flow crosses ~1,000
trades / 24h on the txyz venue — the same census threshold barriers cleared.
